# ChangePrintArea

Umarnin `ChangePrintArea` yana saita yankin murabba'i da [Print Manager](../print-manager/) ke fitarwa. Yana aiki a kan zane yayin da Print Manager ke ɓoye, kuma yana karɓar kusurwoyi biyu masu fuskantar juna — dannawa biyu iri ɗaya da [Rectangle](../rectangle/), don haka rubuta madaidaita da kama suna aiki daidai.

## Zaɓar yanki

1. Rubuta `ChangePrintArea` a tasha, ko danna **Change Area** a mashigin gefen Print Manager. Print Manager zai ɓoye kuma zanen zai zama mai amsawa.
2. **Danna kusurwa ta farko**, ko rubuta `X,Y` sannan ka danna **Enter** don madaidaicin wuri.
3. **Danna kusurwar da ke fuskantar ta**, ko sake rubuta `X,Y`.

Print Manager zai sake buɗewa da sabon yanki a cikin preview, wanda ke canza girma zuwa daidai gwargwadon yankin.

Kusurwoyi suna kama da riko da mahaɗai kamar kowane zaɓin maki, don haka za ka iya yanke bisa siffar da aka zana maimakon ƙiyasi da ido. Tsarin kusurwoyin biyu ba shi da muhimmanci: kusurwoyi masu fuskantar juna suna bayyana murabba'i ɗaya.

Danna `Escape` don sokewa. Ba a rubuta komai ba, don haka Print Manager zai sake buɗewa da yankin da yake da shi tuni.

## Inda ake tuna yankin

Ana adana zaɓin bisa kowane yanayi, ba gaba ɗaya ba:

| Yanayi | Wuri |
|---|---|
| Sararin model | Wuri ɗaya da ake rabawa |
| Kowane shimfida | Wurinsa na kansa, ana ajiye shi daban |

Sake buɗe Print Manager a kan shimfida ɗaya — ko a Model — yana mayar da yankin ƙarshe na wannan yanayin maimakon sake saita shi, kuma canzawa tsakanin shimfidu yana barin yankin kowanne kamar yadda yake.

Ana ajiye wannan a ƙwaƙwalwa kawai. Sake loda shafin yana share duk yankunan da aka ajiye, sannan Print Manager ya koma ga tsoffin ƙimomin da ke ƙasa.

## Yankin tsoho

Idan babu abin da aka ajiye don yanayin yanzu, Print Manager yana buɗewa a:

| Yanayi | Tsoho |
|---|---|
| Sararin model | Akwatin da ya kewaye dukkan abubuwa — girman guda da [Fit](../fit/) ke kusantowa |
| Kowane shimfida | Duk takardar |
