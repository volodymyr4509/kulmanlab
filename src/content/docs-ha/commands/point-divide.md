# Point Divide

Umarnin `RabaDaƊigo` yana sanya [maki](../point/) da ke raba abu zuwa ɓangarori masu tsawo daidai. Abin kansa ba ya canjawa: makin sababbin abubuwa ne a kan layer da launi na yanzu, masu amfani don maƙalawa.

## Raba abu

1. Rubuta `RabaDaƊigo` a tashar umarni ko ka zaɓi **Point Divide** daga jerin zaɓin array a kwamitin Edit.
2. **Danna abin** — layi, polyline, baka, da'ira, ellipse ko spline. Ana ƙin duk wani abu daban, kuma za ka iya sake zaɓe.
3. **Rubuta adadin ɓangarori**, cikakkiyar lamba daga 2 zuwa 32767. Ana ganin makin a kan abin yayin da kake rubutu.
4. Danna **Enter** ko **Space** don sanya su. Umarnin ya ƙare.

## Inda makin ke zuwa

- Ana auna tazara da tsawo tare da abin, ba da kusurwa ba, don haka ana yanka ellipse ko spline zuwa guntaye masu tsawo daidai. Ana bin ɓangarorin baka na polyline tare da lanƙwasarsu.
- A kan abu a buɗe — layi, polyline a buɗe, baka, ɓangaren ellipse ko spline a buɗe — makin suna tsakanin ɓangarorin, ba a ƙarshe ba: ɓangarori 5 suna ba da maki 4.
- A kan abu a rufe — da'ira, polyline a rufe, cikakken ellipse ko spline a rufe — makin suna zagaye gaba ɗaya daga farkonsa, wanda shi ma yake samun maki: da'ira tana farawa a kusurwa 0, polyline a rufe a vertex ɗinta na farko. ɓangarori 5 suna ba da maki 5.
- Ana ƙin lamba a wajen 2–32767: tashar umarni tana faɗa dalili kuma tana share ta don ka sake rubutawa.

Backspace yana share lamba ta ƙarshe. Escape yana sake farawa, don ka zaɓi wani abu. Enter ko Space kafin a zaɓi abu yana gama umarnin.
