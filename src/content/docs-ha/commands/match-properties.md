# Match Properties

Umarnin `KwafaHalaye` yana kwafin **abubuwan gani da layer** daga abu na tushe zuwa abu ɗaya ko fiye na manufa. Kawai abubuwan da ake raba tsakanin nauʼukan abu na tushe da manufa ana canja su — geometry ba ta taɓa canzawa ba.

## Yadda ake kunnawa

Danna maɓallin kayan aiki na **Match Properties** (alamar rolar fenti) a panel na Style, ko rubuta `KwafaHalaye` a tashar umarni.

## Tsarin aiki

**Kunna tukuna, sannan zaɓi tushe:**

1. Rubuta `KwafaHalaye` ko danna maɓallin kayan aiki ba tare da zaɓi mai riga ba.
2. **Danna abin tushe** — wanda kake son kwafin abubuwansa.
3. **Danna kowane abin manufa** don yin amfani da abubuwan tushe. Za ka iya danna abubuwa masu yawa ɗaya bayan wani.
4. Don yin amfani da ƙungiya lokaci ɗaya, **ja akwatin zaɓi** a kan manufofin.
5. Danna **Enter** ko **Escape** don kammalawa.

**Zaɓi tushe tukuna, sannan kunna:**

1. Danna abu ɗaya don zaɓen shi.
2. Kunna `KwafaHalaye`. Ana amfani da abin da aka zaɓa a matsayin tushe kai tsaye.
3. Danna abubuwan manufa ko ja-zaɓi, sannan **Enter** ko **Escape** don kammalawa.

## Wace abubuwa ake kwafi

MatchProperties yana kwafi abubuwan da suke na aji na tushe da ake raba tsakanin tushe da manufa. A mafi ƙaranci, **dukkan nauʼukan abu** suna raba waɗannan abubuwan:

| Abu | Bayani |
|----------|-------------|
| **Launi** | Alamar launi na abu (ciki har da "By Layer" / "By Block") |
| **Layer** | Layer da abin ke ciki |

Idan tushe da manufa nauʼin abu ɗaya ne (misali dukkansu girma-girma ne), ana kuma kwafin ƙarin abubuwan na musamman ga nauʼin — misali tsayin rubutu, girman kibiya, saitunan layin extension.

Geometry (daidaitawa, radius, tsawo, da sauransu) ba ta taɓa shafarwa ba.

## Marfe na maɓallan madannai

| Maɓalli | Aiki |
|-----|--------|
| `Enter` / `Space` | Tabbatar da zaɓen yanki ko kammala umarnin |
| `Escape` | Kammala amfani da (idan tushe an saita) ko soke |

## Bayanan hali

- Ba a taɓa gyara abin tushe da kansa ba.
- Kowace dannawa ko ja-zaɓi yana amfani da abubuwan tushe nan take — babu wani mataki na tabbatarwa.
- Zaɓen yanki yana bin dokoki na yau da kullum: ja **dama** don zaɓi mai tsauri (an rufe gaba ɗaya), ja **hagu** don zaɓi mai haɗuwa (kowace mahaɗa).
- Dannawa abin tushe a matsayin manufa ana yin banza da shi.
- Ga abubuwa masu rubutu (**Text**, **Dimensions**, **Multileaders**), tsayin rubutu kaɗai ake kwafi — font, mai-nauyi, karkatacce, da wasu saitunan salon rubutu ba a daidaita su ba.
