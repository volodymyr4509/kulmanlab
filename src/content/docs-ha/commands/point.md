# Point

Umarnin `Ɗigo` yana sanya maki a kowane zaɓi kuma yana ci gaba da aiki har sai ka danna Enter, Space ko Escape. Maki wuri ne ba tare da girma ba: ana zana shi a matsayin ƙaramin digo kuma yana ɗauke da layer da launi na yanzu. Ana adana shi a matsayin abin `POINT`.

## Sanya maki

1. Rubuta `Ɗigo` a tashar umarni ko ka danna **Point**, maɓalli na ƙarshe a kwamitin Shapes.
2. **Danna** inda maki zai je, ko ka rubuta `X,Y` ka danna **Enter** don ainihin wuri. Kowane zaɓi yana sanya maki kuma tashar umarni tana rubuta haɗin kansa; umarnin yana ci gaba da aiki don na gaba.
3. Ko kuma ka kulle alkibla daga [fil na vector](../../interface/vector-pins/) ka rubuta nisa — maki yana sauka daidai nisan nan tare da ita.
4. Danna **Enter**, **Space** ko **Escape** don gamawa. Enter da Space suna gamawa ne kawai idan ba a rubuta komai ba.

## Yadda maki yake kama

Ana zana maki a matsayin digo mai radius 1.5 px a kowane matakin zuƙowa, don haka yana ci gaba da bayyana idan ka zuƙo waje kuma ba ya girma idan ka zuƙo ciki. Ba a karanta salon maki na zane (`$PDMODE` da `$PDSIZE`) ba tukuna, don haka kowane maki digo ne, duk abin da DXF ya ce.

## Gyara maki

Maki da aka zaɓa yana da riƙo ɗaya, wanda ke motsa shi. Zaɓin akwati yana ɗaukar maki idan yana cikin akwatin, ta kowace alkiblar ja.

[Move](../move/), [Copy](../copy/), [Rotate](../rotate/), [Mirror](../mirror/) da [Scale](../scale/) suna aiki a kan maki kamar kowane abu. Taga kaddarori tana nuna Position X da Position Y, tare da launi da layer.

## DXF — abin POINT

Ana karanta maki daga DXF kuma ana rubuta su a matsayin abubuwan `POINT` tare da wuri, launi da layer (lambobin rukuni 10/20, 62 da 8), kuma suna tafiya ta JSON da aka adana. A da ana tsallake su a shigo da fayil. [Export Manager](../export-manager/) yana lissafa Points a layi nasu, don haka za ka iya barin su daga fitarwa.
